## Post text (personal version, recommended)

For most of my 25 years leading IT teams, I proudly told anyone who would listen that I was the worst manager ever.

I meant it and I was proud of it. 😄

I never wanted to be a manager. I wanted to be a leader, and those turned out to be very different things. Some of you watched me learn that the hard way.

So I finally wrote it all down. The wins, the failures, the HR meeting that went sideways, the story of Gary, and the real reason I retired early.

It's all at **WhyPeopleFollow.com**, along with a free newsletter called **Worth Following**: one honest leadership story every other Tuesday.

If you lead people, hope to someday, or have ever worked for a bad boss, I'd love for you to subscribe. And if you know someone who just got promoted into management, please share this with them. 🙏

👉 https://www.whypeoplefollow.com

You can't make someone follow you. But you can become someone worth following.

## Tips

- Ask friends to share the post in the text itself. That's how it reaches people you don't know.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Facebook shows the post to more people when it gets early conversation.
- A week later, post again with a different hook. For example, link to the Gary story:
  "Ever had your brilliant new hire turn on you? Here's what happened with Gary…"
  https://www.whypeoplefollow.com/articles/trusting-high-performers-story-of-gary
